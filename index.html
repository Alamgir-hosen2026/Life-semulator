Privacy Policy for Life Simulator One

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Alamgir App Studio ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information in connection with the Life Simulator One mobile application (the "App"). By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Life Simulator One does not require you to create an account or sign in. All information described below is entered voluntarily by you directly into the App in order for it to function.

1.1 Profile Information

  • Age
  • Country
  • Relationship status
  • Number of children

1.2 Financial Information

  • Monthly and annual income
  • Savings balance
  • Debt balance
  • Rent or mortgage amount
  • Monthly expenses
  • Current investment balances
  • Financial goals (free text you enter)
  • Custom "what-if" scenario details (scenario names, income/expense assumptions)
  • Goal-tracking data (target and current amounts for categories such as Buy Home, Buy Car, Clear Debt, Education, Vacation, Emergency Fund, and Retirement)
  • Couple Mode information, if used (partner names, individual income/expenses, shared goals, house plan, and retirement plan notes you choose to enter)

Important: All figures and projections you see in the App (Buy vs Rent, Investment Projection, Retirement Simulator, Inflation Simulator, etc.) are calculated locally on your device from the numbers above. We do not verify, audit, or use this information for any purpose other than powering the calculators and dashboard inside the App.

1.3 Purchase Information

If you subscribe to Life Simulator One, your subscription purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We may receive limited, non-sensitive purchase status information (such as whether your subscription is active) from Google Play in order to unlock the App's features.

1.4 Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for advertising purposes. The App does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

  • Local storage: All data you enter is first saved locally on your device.
  • Cloud backup: If you choose to use the "Back Up Data" feature, your data is uploaded to a Firebase Realtime Database (a service provided by Google) under a randomly generated, anonymous Backup Code. This code is not linked to your name, email, or any other personally identifying account information.
  • Restoring data: Entering a Backup Code from another device will download and overwrite the data on your current device. You are responsible for keeping your Backup Code safe, as anyone with the code can access the data associated with it.
  • PDF Reports: If you use the "Export Report" feature, a PDF summary is generated and stored temporarily in the App's private cache folder on your device so it can be shared or saved using your device's normal share options. This file is not uploaded to our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used solely to:

  • Calculate and display your financial dashboard, scenarios, and projections
  • Allow you to back up and restore your data across devices
  • Provide the core features of the App (goal tracking, comparisons, reports, etc.)

We do not use your financial information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App's core functionality.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information to third parties. Your data may be processed by the following service providers strictly to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase (Realtime Database) — used to store your backed-up data. See Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play Billing — used to process subscription payments. See Google's Privacy Policy at the link above.

We do not share your data with any other third party except where required by law.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data through your device settings. Data backed up to Firebase remains associated with your Backup Code until you request its deletion. To request deletion of your backed-up data, please contact us using the details below and provide your Backup Code.

6. Children's Privacy

Life Simulator One is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures, including Firebase security rules, to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

  • You may use the App without ever enabling cloud backup.
  • You may edit or delete any information you have entered at any time within the App.
  • You may uninstall the App at any time to remove all locally stored data from your device.

9. Disclaimer

All calculators, projections, and simulations in Life Simulator One (including Investment Projection, Retirement Simulator, Buy vs Rent, Inflation Simulator, and all other tools) produce hypothetical, straight-line estimates based solely on the figures and assumptions you enter. They are not guaranteed outcomes and do not constitute financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. Please consult a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date." Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
